# Controlled Inference Business Testing Verification Evidence

**Date:** 2026-09-14

## Delivered behavior

- `portrait` defaults to the `controlled_inference` execution profile.
- `facts_only` and strict audit claims retain the `strict` execution profile.
- Chart facts must come from a qualified external calculation capability; the
  language model may neither calculate nor repair them.
- Controlled interpretation uses typed, source-anchored claims and
  `long-form-personality-book-v1`: front matter, prologue, four parts, all
  fifty-six chapter dimensions, finale, audit appendix, explicit limitations,
  and inference disclosure.
- Safe unsupported details are omitted and reported as `CONFIG_LIMITATION`;
  an unanchored, promoted, or overclaiming interpretation is fatal
  `INFERENCE_GUARD_ERROR`.
- Business testing compares the same case across at least two agents for fact
  fidelity, anchor coverage, framework adherence, disclosure completeness,
  section completeness, and harmful overclaiming. Identical prose is not a
  requirement.
- Passing the business-test checklist does not certify strict production.

## Test-first evidence

- Execution-profile routing test failed before the profile branch existed and
  passed after the Skill and gate checklists were updated.
- Controlled-inference contract test failed before its schema and checklist
  existed and passed after the fact and claim boundaries were implemented.
- Portrait-report contract test failed before its schema and checklist existed
  and passed after the fixed report structure and Execution Report fields were
  implemented.
- Failure-policy test failed before profile-specific warning and fatal behavior
  existed and passed after the policy, methodology index, and runtime rules
  were aligned.
- Business-status and rubric tests failed before the checklist and manifest
  fields existed and passed after all authoritative documents were aligned.
- A final ambiguity test failed while legacy status text treated the strict
  calculation gate as universal; it passed after the text was explicitly
  scoped to the strict profile.
- The first live portrait exposed that generic summary sections did not match
  the approved long-form reference report. Structural tests failed first, then
  passed after the four-part, fifty-six-chapter content blueprint, finale, and
  audit appendix were added without copying case conclusions into the Skill.
- A later depth regression exposed that structural coverage alone allowed
  summary-length chapters. The contract now requires at least three distinct
  analytical moves per supported chapter and rejects paraphrase padding.
