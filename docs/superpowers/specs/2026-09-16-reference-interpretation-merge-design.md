# Reference Interpretation Merge Design

## Goal

Preserve the strongest insights from the approved reference report and the
controlled portrait without promoting reference-only traditional conclusions
to project-verified facts.

## Claim layers

The merged report keeps three visible assurance layers:

1. `calculated_fact`: chart positions and aspects returned by the qualified
   runtime capabilities.
2. `controlled_inference`: personality interpretation anchored to calculated
   facts under the existing controlled profile.
3. `reference_traditional_interpretation`: a traditional conclusion supplied
   by a user-approved reference report but not independently certified by the
   project's missing strict configuration.

Reference interpretations may enrich prose only when they do not contradict
the accepted calculation facts. They retain their source and limitation in the
audit appendix and never become `project_verified` through repetition.

## Report merge

Add the reference report's useful emphasis on traditional Bazi strength and
balancing logic, Mercury domicile, Mars exaltation, identity continuity,
interpersonal effects of high standards, and authorship of one's life story.
Keep the controlled report's historical-time transparency, concrete lived
contexts, behavioral verification, small-step commitment, and practical
recalibration.

Use `有根的远行者｜有根的探索者` as the dual archetype. `远行者` describes
the life movement between rootedness and possibility; `探索者` describes the
cognitive movement between inquiry and grounded delivery.

## Validation

The Skill contract and checklist must require provenance, non-promotion, and
conflict handling for reference-derived interpretations. The sample report
must retain all 56 chapters, three-to-five paragraph depth, explicit
assurance, and an updated omission list. The PDF must be regenerated and
visually sampled at the front matter, technical chapters, integrated portrait,
and audit appendix.

## Scope

No reference PDF or calculation software is packaged into the Skill. No strict
Bazi strength, favorable-element, dignity, or predictive certification is
claimed. The reference remains an execution-time, user-approved source.
