# Reader-First Editorial Design

## Goal

Turn the merged controlled portrait into an immersive personality book while
retaining every fact, traditional interpretation, limitation, and provenance
record in the audit layer.

## Reader-facing rule

The body may state an accepted traditional interpretation in natural language.
It must not narrate internal approval, source identifiers, configuration gates,
project verification state, or assurance mechanics. Those details belong in
the audit appendix. A brief front-matter note may route readers to the appendix
without interrupting the narrative.

## Narrative hierarchy

Use `有根的远行者` as the single dominant archetype. Use `有根的探索者` only
as its cognitive subtype: the method by which the traveler explores, verifies,
returns, and builds.

Differentiate the recurring agency theme as a progression:

1. judgment right;
2. expression ownership;
3. choice and life authorship;
4. interpretation right;
5. public authorship.

## Practical guidance

Keep useful calibration, but render it as a description of mature expression
rather than repeated instructions. Concrete procedures remain only where they
materially clarify the pattern. Do not reduce any supported chapter below the
three-layer depth contract.

## Validation

The Skill contract must explicitly route source qualification to the audit
appendix. The sample body must contain no internal workflow phrases. The audit
appendix must retain `reference_traditional_interpretation`, source, approval,
compatibility, limitations, and fact assurance. The regenerated PDF requires
visual sampling of the cover, traditional-interpretation chapters, archetype,
finale, and audit pages.
