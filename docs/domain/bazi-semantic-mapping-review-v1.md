## Boundary and evidence standard

These are review hypotheses, not rules in `bazi_mapping_registry_v1.yaml`.
They use only fact families that the project methodology names: day-master
environment, Ten-God relations, stem/branch relations, and explicit structural
context. They do not establish canonical tokens, strength calculations,
thresholds, weights, or direction values; those require approved vocabulary,
deterministic tables, and a later mapping asset.

Every future Bazi rule must require at least one contextual qualifier, preserve
counterevidence, and state an exclusion. No isolated Heavenly Stem, Earthly
Branch, Ten God, combination, clash, or apparent structure can determine a
Primitive alone. Luck cycles and annual transits are out of scope.

## Cross-cutting modifiers proposed for C2 review

- **Context locality:** record whether the evidence concerns self-concept,
  decision, relationship, work, or pressure context; do not generalize it.
- **Seasonal/environmental qualifier:** any future strength-sensitive rule must
  cite an approved day-master/environment calculation, never model judgment.
- **Relation qualifier:** combinations, clashes, harms, punishments, and other
  relations can qualify a proposition only when their deterministic fact shape
  and semantic effect are separately approved.
- **Conflict handling:** Bazi-internal conflict yields `mixed` or a limitation;
  it is not resolved by later Astrology evidence.

## C2 review decisions required

For each proposition, the product reviewer must accept, revise, or reject the
semantic bridge before a machine-readable candidate exists. Acceptance must
name permitted fact families, required context, prohibited shortcuts, and the
counterevidence that later State Resolution must retain.
