# C2-SM P004 Bazi Dao-Shi Method Reconstruction Final Report

Date: 2026-10-03

## Source corpus

The review retained the existing Tier-A classical source records for *Yuanhai Ziping* and *Sanming Tonghui*. No modern web summary was promoted into method authority. Repeated transcriptions were treated as access witnesses rather than independent evidence.

## What the sources establish

The reviewed passages establish all of the following:

- Eating God must exist; without it, Indirect Resource is only interpreted as Indirect Resource.
- The core explanation is directional: an Indirect Resource element controls the Eating God element.
- Strength is material; terms and examples distinguish strong/weak Day Master, flourishing/declining Food God, and strong Indirect Resource.
- Wealth, restraint, and combinations can alter or reverse the adverse judgement.
- Hidden stems, month context, and concrete chart structure can participate.
- A direct warning forbids treating every observed Indirect Resource/Food God pairing as an adverse result.

These findings reject the legacy shortcut `Eating God + Indirect Resource -> Dao-Shi`.

## What remains unavailable

The sources do not provide one reproducible procedure for:

1. selecting the operative Eating God among multiple instances;
2. computing strength from season, roots, visibility, and competing forces;
3. deciding when raw control becomes effective control;
4. determining whether Wealth has sufficient reach and force to rescue;
5. generalizing the listed combination exceptions;
6. ordering exceptions and resolving conflicts;
7. pairing and aggregating multiple Food Gods and Indirect Resources.

No point system or modern reconstruction was invented to fill these gaps.

## Exceptions and rescue

The classical list proves that exceptions are method material, not optional decoration. It also proves that `wealth exists != rescue` and `combination exists != exception resolved`. The current evidence supports a registry of source examples, but not a total executable exception engine.

## Strength and whole-chart dependency

Strength is required by the text but lacks a single governed computation. The method is therefore `BLOCKED_BY_STRENGTH_METHODOLOGY` and by unresolved whole-chart precedence. Neutral season, position, visibility, and relation facts can supply inputs; they cannot decide strength.
