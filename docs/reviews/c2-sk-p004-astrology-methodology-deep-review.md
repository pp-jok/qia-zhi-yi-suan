# C2-SK P004 Astrology Methodology Deep Review

## School scope

The available evidence is traditional western / horary-informed symbolic interpretation. It does not select a universal natal school, and this review does not mix it with modern psychological or popular astrology. The Mars source gives semantic relevance—action and initiative—and explicitly says dignity and aspects reveal particulars. It does not supply a condition-complete natal P004 high rule.

## Qualifier findings

- **Mars:** action/initiative is direct relevance, but significator is not direction. Conflict, assertion, survival response, courage, impulse and force remain distinct constructs.
- **Dignity:** the runtime calculates basic essential dignities. The reviewed methodology uses them to qualify expression, not to establish the time or manner concrete action starts. Strong/effective is not P004 high.
- **Aspect:** body, type and orb exist; the proposed Gate is still only admission. Ease/tension, conjunction/square/trine/sextile/opposition do not map mechanically to high/low.
- **Sect:** absent from the Canonical Fact model. Its possible impact is school-specific quality/malefic assessment, not demonstrated P004 direction.
- **Application/separation:** absent and primarily evidenced in the current corpus as technique/timing discourse rather than stable natal P004 semantics.
- **Domination/dexter-sinister:** absent and not demonstrated material to P004 under an unselected natal methodology.

## Direction and counterevidence

There is no source-bounded rule explaining why any condition means concrete action is readily initiated and actively advanced rather than merely energetic, assertive, capable, conflicting, or effective. There is also no independent traditional rule that turns Saturn, delay, discipline, difficulty, or Mars-Saturn tension into P004 low. Such conditions may at most become future counterevidence after a school and construct-specific rule is selected; they are not opposite-direction primary evidence.

## Result

Astrology high is `PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_NOT_YET_FEASIBLE`: it is blocked by D2 Root pending, natal-school selection, synthesis policy, and direction/counterevidence semantics—not merely by computation. Astrology low is `NO_DEFENSIBLE_MECHANISM` in the current scope.
