## Scope

This audit reopens only P004 advancement. Initiation and pre-action Low remain saturated. The search is limited to classical start-to-continuation language and does not reopen courage, haste, decisiveness, caution, Seven Killings or Yang Blade.

## Authority and digital witness policy

The authority is the identified classical work and edition. Wikimedia Commons scans, Wikisource transcriptions and searchable OCR sites are access representations, not independent traditional authorities. A transcription is never assigned Tier A merely because it appears on Wikisource.

## Direct-equivalent search result

The standalone phrases `始勤終惰` and `有始無終` remain nearby diligence/persistence or completion language. The materially new evidence is the longer source sentence `作事進退悔懶、有始無終`, independently preserved in two classical works. It explicitly binds the phrase to doing matters and to advance/retreat during the process.

Search saturation is reached for this round. Repeated phrases without `作事` or equivalent process ownership were not accumulated as votes.
