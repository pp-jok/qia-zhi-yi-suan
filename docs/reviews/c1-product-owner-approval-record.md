## Normative context-promotion rules

- Two labels are not two independent contexts. Independence must inspect evidence root, fact reference, mapping rule, and context origin.
- A shared evidence root cannot count as two independent context supports.
- Promotion produces only `global_candidate_evidence`; Primitive State Resolution alone may resolve `supported_high`, `supported_low`, `mixed`, or `unknown`.
- A material counter-context is independently sourced, qualified, representative, and direction-opposed local evidence. It blocks promotion and preserves contextual variation.
- Local high plus local low is not global mixed. Global mixed is restricted to unresolved, qualified conflict within global-scope evidence.
- Quantity within one context never substitutes for context coverage.
