# C2-SK P004 Astrology Authority Source Corpus

## Accepted

| Source | School / scope | Authority use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demetra George, *Ancient Astrology in Theory and Practice*, Vols. I–II (Rubedo, 2019/2022) | Reconstructed Hellenistic natal | Coherent natal planetary-condition and delineation method | Establishes method, not P004 direction. |
| Deborah Houlding, *Understanding Planetary Dignity and Debility* | Traditional western technical | Essential/accidental condition distinction | Predictive/horary examples cannot be silently imported into natal direction. |
| Nicholas Campion, *Introduction to Aspects and Chart Shaping in Natal Astrology* (2003) | Modern western natal | Natal aspect combination, moderation, harmony/tension and orb | Introductory; does not create high/low. |
| Deborah Houlding, Mars and classical-aspect articles | Traditional / horary-informed | Mars relevance and historical technique boundaries | Existing claims remain school-specific and non-directional. |

## Deferred or rejected

- Modern psychological natal astrology: deferred for P004 method selection because the reviewed material supplies symbolic/psychological constructs but no reproducible condition-complete direction rule.
- Renaissance/horary perfection, application and reception technique: non-natal for this use unless a natal authority makes the transfer explicit.
- SEO, anonymous, popular sign tables, AI summaries and content farms: rejected.

No accepted source is empirical psychological evidence.
