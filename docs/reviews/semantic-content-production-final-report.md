# Semantic Content Production Final Report

Release target: `v0.4.1`

Decision:
`ALL_REOPENED_CORE_PATHS_DEEP_RESEARCH_SATURATED__NO_FORMAL_RUNTIME_ACTIVATION`

## Executive result

P001, P002, P003, P005, and P006 were reopened beyond the previous
repository-only review. Each Primitive received an independent Bazi and
Hellenistic source search, construct-first classification, method and canonical
fact feasibility review, counterevidence review, and explicit Root/PE/Mapping
gate decision. P004 remained frozen.

No candidate cleared both necessary gates:

1. it must directly answer the approved Primitive semantic question rather
   than a nearby role, capability, temperament, intensity, outcome, or
   structural property; and
2. it must be reproducible from governed canonical facts through a source-faithful
   method with explicit aggregation, exceptions, and counterevidence.

The formal result therefore remains six `unknown` states, zero active Mapping
records, and zero downstream formations. This is a stronger evidentiary basis
for `unknown`, not a non-zero personality feature.

## Research products

- Shared Bazi and Hellenistic source-corpus records separate source authority,
  access representation, and empirical-status limitations.
- Five deep construct matrices compare both directions independently and record
  adjacent meanings rather than promoting them.
- Five expanded final reports record the complete Source -> Construct -> Method
  -> Fact -> Root -> PE -> Mapping analysis.
- `primitive_coverage_v2.yaml` replaces the package loader's repository-only v1
  basis with a deep-independent-research terminal status while preserving P004.

## Why P006 was not activated

P006 was the strongest discovery path and therefore received priority. Lexical
closeness did not resolve the contract mismatch. The Bazi phrases depend on
undefined or ungoverned conditions such as whole-chart strength, subject
selection, and rule precedence; they also speak to ability or result. Ptolemy's
“systematic workers” occurs inside a broader soul delineation and describes a
work capability. Neither source establishes a stable high/low task-arrangement
preference under the approved ontology.

Activating P006 would therefore require one of three prohibited moves: treating
capability as orientation, inventing missing calculation rules, or redefining
P006. The release does none of them.

## Runtime and trust-boundary impact

The mature formal runtime was not redesigned. It now loads the v2 coverage
manifest, continues to require the governed active Mapping bundle, and rejects
non-terminal or mismatched closure semantics. Candidate Preview and legacy
assets remain unable to supply formal release candidates.

The previous v1 manifest remains historical package data; it is not the loader
authority. The authoritative v2 manifest records no active PE or Mapping refs.

## Version classification

This release is `v0.4.1`, not `v0.5.0`. A `v0.5.0` classification remains
reserved for all of the following:

- at least one approved PRIMARY_EVIDENCE record;
- at least one active Mapping bound to that evidence;
- at least one formal Core Primitive with a non-`unknown` result;
- unchanged trust, package, and regression gates.

## Closure

The current Semantic Core foundation should stop searching for incremental
infrastructure work. Future work may reopen a Primitive only with a materially
new direct construct and a reproducible method/fact path. It must not reopen the
same nearby-language candidates merely to pursue a non-zero profile.
