reproducible construct for P001, P002, P003, P005, or P006. It does not treat a
traditional doctrine as empirical personality validation. Every claim below is

## Ptolemaic method reconstruction

III.13 does not assign a modern personality dimension from one isolated fact.
It directs the reader to examine Mercury and the Moon, the signs containing
them, their rulers and dominance, the Sun and angles, planetary natures and
mixtures, and modifying conditions. The chapter's character phrases are thus
outputs of an integrated judgement procedure.

The current deterministic fact contract does not govern all inputs needed to
reproduce that procedure. Material gaps include traditional rulership and
dominance selection, sect, application/separation, stations, visibility and
phase, terms and triplicity, and a rule-complete precedence scheme. The current
Astrology configuration's tropical/Placidus and modern-planet choices are also
not by themselves a reconstruction of III.13.

conditions. A modern source that recasts those words as a Big Five trait or a
work-style preference would change the school and construct. It cannot be used
solely because the selected Hellenistic corpus did not yield a non-zero result.
