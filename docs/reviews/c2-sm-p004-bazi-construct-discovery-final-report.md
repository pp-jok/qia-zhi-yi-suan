## 7. Direct initiation constructs

## 8. Advancement constructs

No qualifying direct construct. “始勤终惰” is the closest process-like phrase, but it is an isolated diligence/completion maxim under undefined `梟印当权`, not a governed P004 mechanism. “有始无终” similarly describes continuity or outcome rather than an auditable advancement rule.

## 9. Pre-action restraint constructs

None. “多三思、少果决”, “作事狐疑”, “临事模棱少决” and “作为多滞” describe indecision, cognitive style, obstruction or inability. They do not state a preference to observe, prepare, restrain or wait before acting. One source explicitly pairs indecision with a desire for speed, which defeats a preferred-waiting interpretation.

## 10. Nearby constructs
