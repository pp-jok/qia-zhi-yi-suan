## Research boundary

This corpus asks whether a reviewed Bazi method owns a technical construct for starting or actively advancing concrete action, or for preferentially observing, preparing, restraining or waiting before action. It does not ask which Ten God resembles a modern personality label.

The original technical meaning and the project P004 interpretation remain separate throughout. A phrase about courage, haste, decisiveness, diligence, success, obstruction or temperament does not become P004 merely because its modern wording sounds behavioral.

## Nearby

- *Yuanhai Ziping* elemental temperament includes “意速心焦”, “有始无终”, “刚毅有决”, “多三思、少果决” and “作事挫志”. These concern haste, completion, decisiveness or frustration, not the P004 start/advance axis.
- Its *Xiang Xin Fu* describes Seven Killings as contentious, fierce and hasty; this is courage/aggression/impulsivity, not initiation.
- “梟印当权，使心机而始勤终惰” is a start-to-finish diligence description, but `梟印当权` is not a standalone audited method construct and diligence/persistence is not the project's pre-action Low pole.
- *Ditian Sui Chanwei* uses “易进难退”, “作事狐疑，少决断”, “作为多滞” and “临事而模棱少决”. These describe firmness, decisional hesitation, obstruction or insufficient realization; none states a preferred period of observation/preparation before concrete action.
- “有斟酌，有变化” and “能决大谋” describe judgement/planning capability, owned by nearby cognition or P001 boundaries rather than P004.

## Structural-only

- 食神生财, 食神制杀, 杀印相生, 伤官佩印, 阳刃透官杀 and other named combinations are pattern relations whose classical function concerns pattern formation, control, office, wealth, reputation or fortune.
- *Ziping Zhenquan* explicitly makes success/failure depend on month command, Four-Pillar placement, conflict, combination, protection and rescue. A component name never supplies the complete judgement.
- 旺衰, 得令, 透藏, 根气, 制化 and 顺逆 describe strength, configuration and method operation. “旺” is not P004 High and “弱” is not P004 Low.

## Outcome-only

Richness, rank, accomplishment, failure, “成格”, “败格”, “发福”, military achievement and success in office are outcomes. They do not describe the stable process by which action starts or advances.

## School-specific

- *Yuanhai Ziping* mixes elemental temperament, Ten-God, special-pattern and verse traditions.
- *Ditian Sui* foregrounds configuration, flow and balance; Ren's commentary adds whole charts and later interpretive detail.
- *Ziping Zhenquan* centers month-command pattern formation and rescue.
- *Sanming Tonghui* is a compendium, not evidence that all included methods are mutually composable.
- Contemporary structural pedagogy adds explanatory organization but cannot retroactively turn a modern personality word into a classical construct.

## Conflicting or non-combinable

The texts differ over method center, pattern use, strength, special configurations and interpretation level. “Seven Killings”, “Output” or “flow” cannot be averaged across them into an authorless action score. Classical verse, later commentary and modern paraphrase remain separate evidence roots.

## Rejected

- 食神/伤官 equals action or execution;
- 七杀/阳刃 equals High because it appears forceful or decisive;
- 印星 equals Low because it appears thoughtful or slow;
- 财星 equals advancement because it is linked with enterprise or results;
- fire/wood equals action and water/metal equals hesitation;
- clash equals impulsivity, combination equals hesitation, control equals self-restraint;
- strength, favorable element, pattern success or useful-god status equals direction;
- luck cycles or annual timing as a substitute for a natal Core Profile construct;
- SEO pages, anonymous formulas, copied keyword lists and modern “Bazi MBTI” labels.

## Saturated

Ten-God identity, multi-factor combinations, month-command pattern method, elemental temperament, whole-chart flow/obstruction and modern structural reading were all reviewed. Repeated hits remained courage, decisiveness, haste, planning, diligence, obstruction, career or outcome. Status: `NEARBY_CONSTRUCT_SATURATED`.
