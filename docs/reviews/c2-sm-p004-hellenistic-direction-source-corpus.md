## Boundary-only

- Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* III.13–14: “active”, “venturesome” and “cautious” are character descriptors. The surrounding method does not turn them into the project's concrete action-start/advance axis.
- Valens, *Anthologies* IV.27: rising/setting “active impulse” belongs to a chronocrator/year-results procedure, so it cannot be transferred automatically into a natal personality preference.
- Existing `SK-AS-MARS-HOULDING-2004`: useful cross-school Mars relevance hypothesis only; it is not authority for the selected Hellenistic method.

## Discovery-only

- Dorotheus of Sidon, *Carmen Astrologicum* I.30, David Pingree translation excerpt [indexed through Attalus](https://www.attalus.org/info/dorotheus.html) and reproduced in an astrology newsletter. The passage suggests courage/agility and condition-specific outcomes, but the accessible chain and wording do not justify a new auditable direction Claim.
- Paulus, Olympiodorus and Rhetorius discovery results were not promoted: accessible excerpts concerned technique, lots, planetary condition, boldness or violence rather than a direct P004 rule.

## Rejected

- Modern pages equating Mars with “drive” or Saturn with procrastination: wrong authority level and generally outside the selected method.
- Horary application/perfection: event-development semantics, already excluded from automatic natal transfer.
- “Mars strong means High”, “Mars afflicted means Low”, “Saturn means Low”, retrograde, dignity, sect or angularity shortcuts: no direct P004 materiality evidence.

## Conflicting or non-combinable material

The reviewed authors do not expose one uniform Mars rule. Ptolemy describes soul character, Valens often reports multi-planet outcomes, Dorotheus gives placement-conditioned delineations, and Firmicus supplies highly specific configurations. Their vocabulary cannot be merged into an authorless “Hellenistic says” rule.

## Saturated

The Ptolemy, Valens and Firmicus paths repeatedly yielded activity, force, rashness, enterprise, conflict, slowness or impediment. None specified how concrete action is started or advanced, and none supplied independent evidence for observation/preparation/restraint before action. Status: `CLAIM_SATURATED`; further Mars synonym search stopped.

## Registry decision

No Source or Claim was added to the semantic registries. The review discovered no new direction, condition, scope or countercondition knowledge with direct P004 ownership. Adding another “Mars is active” Claim would violate the anti-synonym and saturation rules.
