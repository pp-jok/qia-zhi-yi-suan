## Methodology proposal

`BMC-BZ-P004-DAO-SHI-V1` is created as `proposed`, `PENDING` and inactive. It preserves the source-bounded method question without inventing a strength score. It cannot be approved by this review.

The minimum future method must define an operative 倒食 relation and its rescues/exceptions. It may not equate “both identities occur” with “倒食成立”, and may not use invented point weights.

## Fact and Root boundary

Current deterministic facts and `ER-BZ-TEN-GOD-INSTANCE-V1` identify Ten-God instances only. Extending that Root to dominance, control, rescue or whole-chart synthesis would silently change its approved scope. The new Root is therefore only a concrete proposal and is not eligible for a semantic mechanism.
