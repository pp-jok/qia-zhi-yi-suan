Date: 2026-09-30

## Research boundary

This round searched for a technical construct before searching for a planet. A source was relevant only if its construct directly described one of these processes:

- starting concrete action;
- actively advancing concrete action;
- preserving observation, preparation, restraint or waiting before action.

Lexical resemblance was insufficient. A construct also had to be a stable natal tendency rather than an event chart, a life-stage timer, an occupation, an outcome, courage, impulse, generic activity, slowness or difficulty.

## Direct P004 corpus

None. No reviewed passage reached `DIRECT_INITIATION`, `DIRECT_ADVANCEMENT` or `DIRECT_PRE_ACTION_RESTRAINT` while also remaining natal and stable.

## Nearby constructs

- `praxis`: profession, pursuit or characteristic occupation; not an action-initiation process.
- Lot of Daimon/Spirit: intention, mental activity, reputation and action outcomes; not initiation or advancement preference.
- Saturnian slowness or impediment: diminished activity/difficulty; not chosen pre-action observation or preparation.
- deliberation, long foresight and consideration: cognitive style vocabulary without an explicit “before concrete action” bridge.
- courage, enterprise, impulse and general activity: already excluded neighbors, not synonyms for P004.

## Event and outcome constructs

- `katarche`: an inception/event chart construct even when translated as “beginning” or “initiative”.
- prediction of the success or failure of enterprises: outcome judgement, not personality direction.
- chronocrators, profections and planetary age periods: temporal activation, not a stable natal preference.
- beginning/middle/end of a matter: event development, not the native's habitual way of starting and advancing action.

## Translation and transmission cautions

- Greek `praxis` can be translated as action, practice, conduct, occupation or work. Its technical chapter context controls; the English word “action” does not grant P004 ownership.
- Greek `katarche` can be translated as beginning, inception or initiative. In the reviewed technical context it names an event/inceptional branch, not the psychological trait “initiative”.
- Dorotheus survives through a Pahlavi/Arabic transmission chain; wording must not be treated as an unqualified Greek technical definition.
- The widely used Riley Valens translation is preliminary. English phrases such as “active impulse” require procedural context and cannot be isolated as trait evidence.

## Rejected paths

- “strong Mars means High”, “afflicted Mars means Low” and “Saturn delay means Low”;
- modern psychological planet keywords without a direct ancient technical construct;
- converting courage, force, enterprise, achievement, rashness or generic activity into initiation;
- converting fear, weakness, low energy, obstruction or slowness into preferred preparation/restraint;
- transferring electional, inceptional, horary, annual or life-stage outcomes into a stable natal personality rule;
- inferring a direction from a canonical fact merely because that fact is calculable.
