# Semantic Bridge Governance Final Report

## Baseline and problem

Baseline `v0.4.1` proved that the formal Core correctly remains empty when
evidence is insufficient. It did not yet make the semantic jump from a
traditional claim to a modern Primitive independently governable. This release
adds that missing Bridge boundary without loosening any downstream gate.

## Policy result

Direct-construct and behavioral-mechanism bridges may advance only after all
ten gates pass. Capability/role, temperament, outcome, and analogical-symbolic
bridges are rejected. High and Low are researched independently; no absent
direction supplies its opposite. Context-bound evidence remains local unless a
separate promotion marks it globally eligible.

## P006 result

The Bazi and astrology pilot rejected every near phrase. The alternatives
capability, composite character judgement, outcome, and temperament explain the
sources better than organizational orientation. Traditional method and
Canonical Fact work therefore stop before Evidence Root or PRIMARY_EVIDENCE.
Counts remain PE `0`, active Mapping `0`, and formal P006 `unknown`.

## Resolver and provenance

The resolver now reports a single-context claim as `context_differentiated`
rather than silently promoting its direction globally. Explicitly promoted or
unscoped evidence can still resolve a global state. Semantic Bridge references
are persisted into Primitive results and the provenance graph.

## Ontology compatibility

The review found a systematic mismatch between several v2 modern abstractions
and the native constructs expressed by the researched traditions. v2 is not
edited or retired. A candidate-only v3 experiment opens `TP001 Task Continuity
Process`; it is method-blocked and runtime-ineligible.

## Test, CI, and release decision

Acceptance tests cover all Bridge classes and gates, approved PRIMARY_EVIDENCE
binding, independent High/Low directions, local-first resolution, provenance,
P006 zero activation, and v3 isolation. GitHub CI remains mandatory on Python
3.9, 3.11, and 3.12 plus isolated wheel verification.

Release `v0.4.2` is approved as:

`SEMANTIC_BRIDGE_GOVERNANCE_COMPLETE__P006_BRIDGE_REJECTED__ONTOLOGY_V3_CANDIDATE_OPENED__NO_FORMAL_ACTIVATION`

This is not approval of any new personality conclusion and does not qualify as
v0.5.0.
