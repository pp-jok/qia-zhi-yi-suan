## Ontology boundary

P005 asks how affect is preferentially held or processed: through boundaries,
structure, or regulation (`high`), or as more open, flowing, and less bounded
(`low`). It excludes affect intensity, pathology, relationship need, and
organizational method. `UNKNOWN` is not emotional absence; qualified opposite
evidence must address affect processing in the same scope.

## Bazi search

The Bazi search examined [*Ditian Sui* 〈性情〉](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh/%E6%BB%B4%E5%A4%A9%E9%AB%93/34), [*Sanming Tonghui* 卷七〈论性情相貌〉](https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=548506&if=gb&remap=gb), [*Yuanhai Ziping* 〈论性情〉](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/%E6%B7%B5%E6%B5%B7%E5%AD%90%E5%B9%B3%E5%A4%A7%E5%85%A8), and [*Ziping Zhenquan* 〈论十干配合性情〉](https://www.vr-d.com/pdf-file/%E5%91%BD%E7%90%86%2F%E5%AD%90%E5%B9%B3%E7%9C%9E%E8%AF%A0%E5%8E%9F%E6%9C%AC.pdf).

Their closest language concerns calmness, agitation, hardness/softness,
intensity, ethical feeling, and five-phase balance or flow. These are states,
temperament, expression, or technical cosmology. They do not specify a
person's preferred procedure for holding, bounding, structuring, or allowing
affect. Five-phase “flow”, “restraint”, or “acceptance” cannot be translated
into open affect flow or psychological regulation. The [*Sanming Tonghui*
scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC416-13jh000156-94145_%E4%B8%89%E5%91%BD%E9%80%9A%E6%9C%83.pdf) is a future edition-collation locator, not a mechanism.
