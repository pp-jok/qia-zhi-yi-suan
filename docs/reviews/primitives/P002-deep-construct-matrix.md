# P002 Deep Construct Matrix

Primitive: `P002` — Predictability Orientation

Status: `NO_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`

versus tolerating or seeking change, openness, and lower certainty. It does
not own organizational method, responsibility, task-planning skill, risk
morality, or chart-level stability. High and low were searched independently.
