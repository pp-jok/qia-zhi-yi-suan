# P006 Deep Construct Matrix

Primitive: `P006` — Organizational Orientation  
Research result: `NO_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`

## Ontology boundary

P006 measures the method used to arrange activities and tasks: an **high**
direction means more order, arrangement, or pre-set structure; a **low**
direction means more flexibility and fewer pre-set structures. It is not the
need for predictability or stability (P002), responsibility, moral control, or
affect regulation. A work-only observation does not establish the global P006
construct.

## Bazi search

The reviewed public access witnesses were [Sanming Tonghui](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E5%91%BD%E9%80%9A%E6%9C%83) and [Ditian Sui](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E6%BB%B4%E5%A4%A9%E9%AB%93), alongside the legacy `BZ-C2B-07` hypothesis (`正印/偏印 + 正官/七杀`). They provide no located, auditable mechanism that distinguishes organizational orientation from P002 predictability preference, responsibility, or status/role descriptions. Neither high nor low P006 has a source-level directional bridge.

## Astrology search

The direct source revisit used [Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* III.13](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/3D*.html). Its inquiry concerns the mind through Mercury and the Moon and requires multiple sign, ruler, aspect, and planetary-nature conditions. The closest lexical leads remain mixed descriptions of character, changeability, practical ability, and occupational capacity. They do not state a reusable high/low construct for arranging tasks, nor do they exclude P002-like stability language. The legacy `AS-C2B-07` (`Mercury placement + Mercury–Saturn aspect <= 8°`) is therefore still a rejected hypothesis, not a mapping.

## Decision

No evidence root, primary evidence record, or mapping is admitted. The admissible outcome for both **high** and **low** is `NO_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`.
