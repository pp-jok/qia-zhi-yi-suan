# P003 Deep Construct Matrix — Relational Responsiveness

Status: `NO_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`

P003 asks how relationship interaction is handled and responded to: whether
relational feeling, response, and coordination are actively prioritized
(`high`), or interaction is handled with a comparatively independent or
lower-response orientation (`low`). It is not sociability volume, affection,
marital outcome, judgement ownership, or an attachment diagnosis.

## Bazi method boundary

The Bazi passages are traditional five-phase or whole-chart temperament
doctrines, not an isolated-ten-god rule for P003. *Sanming Tonghui* qualifies
five-phase descriptions with prosperity/decline, restraint and other chart
conditions; *Ziping Zhenquan* uses technical combinations rather than a
behavioural interaction protocol. A public scanned locator for *Sanming
Tonghui* is [the National Library of China copy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC416-13jh000156-94145_%E4%B8%89%E5%91%BD%E9%80%9A%E6%9C%83.pdf), and a
scanned *Yuanhai Ziping* locator is [volume one](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SSID-13003376_%E6%B7%B5%E6%B5%B7%E5%AD%90%E5%B9%B3_%E5%8D%B71.pdf). The searchable
transcriptions support discovery and chapter location; a registry admission
would still need a frozen edition and exact scan/transcription collation.

The rejected legacy Bazi family is independently recorded as `BZ-C2B-04`:
broad two-group Ten-God presence plus a relationship label has no mechanism
from canonical fact to relational attunement. It remains `NO_MAPPING`, rather
than a weak `high` candidate.

## Astrology method boundary

Ptolemy III.13 is the closest selected Hellenistic source because it addresses
qualities of the soul. It explicitly requires a compound judgement involving
Mercury, Moon, signs, rulers, configurations, angles, planetary natures, and
mixtures. Its relationship-adjacent adjectives are therefore neither a direct
P003 construct nor a one-fact rule. The existing Moon/Venus legacy family is
likewise recorded as `AS-C2B-04` `NO_MAPPING`: affection and emotionality do
not establish relational attunement.
