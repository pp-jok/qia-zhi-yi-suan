# P005 Deep Construct Matrix — Affect Regulation Orientation

Status: `NO_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`

P005 asks how affect is preferentially held or processed: through boundaries,
structure, or regulation (`high`), or as more open, flowing, and less bounded
(`low`). It excludes affect intensity, pathology, relationship need, and general
organizational method.

## Bazi method boundary

Five-phase flow, restraint, acceptance, or collection is cosmological and
technical vocabulary. It cannot be silently translated into psychological
containment, self-regulation, or willingness to let affect flow. Fire's
agitation and metal's intensity are excluded affect intensity, not P005
directions. *Sanming Tonghui* conditions its five-phase temperament language on
chart-wide prosperity, decline and restraint; it is not a count-based
one-element method. A scanned [*Sanming Tonghui* copy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC416-13jh000156-94145_%E4%B8%89%E5%91%BD%E9%80%9A%E6%9C%83.pdf) and [*Yuanhai Ziping* volume](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SSID-13003376_%E6%B7%B5%E6%B5%B7%E5%AD%90%E5%B9%B3_%E5%8D%B71.pdf) are retained for future edition collation.

The legacy resource-plus-authority rule `BZ-C2B-06` is `NO_MAPPING`:
pressure, constraint, or structural symbolism cannot establish affect
regulation. It must not be revived by calling its output containment.

## Astrology method boundary

Ptolemy III.13 supplies a quality-of-soul vocabulary superficially close to
restraint or passion. It derives compound character delineations from Mercury,
Moon, signs, rulers, configurations, angles, natural qualities, and mixtures;
it does not state a stable construct of preferred affect holding,
boundary-setting, or processing. The legacy Moon/Mercury rule `AS-C2B-06`
therefore remains `NO_MAPPING`: emotion-related symbolism is not affect
regulation.
