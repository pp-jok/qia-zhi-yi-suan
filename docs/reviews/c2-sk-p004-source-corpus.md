## Project methodology

`SK-PROJECT-P004-ONTOLOGY-V2` is Tier A project methodology. It supplies only P004's boundary: action start/advancement, not energy, assertion, impulsivity, achievement, judgement ownership, predictability, or organization.

## Rejected or unresolved sources

- SEO blogs, anonymous pages, social posts, short video, AI summaries, and popular "Ten-God personality" / sign-personality shorthand: rejected as PRIMARY_EVIDENCE inputs.
- Classical passages not available in a stable edition with a directly locatable P004 claim: unresolved; they may be added only through the same registry and review process.
- No empirical research source was used; the repository must not characterize these claims as scientifically proven personality facts.
