## 3. Research question

The research question remained: **When and how is concrete action started and actively advanced, or held in observation, preparation, restraint or waiting before action?**

High and Low were tested independently. High could not inherit evidence from activity, courage, impulse, achievement or visibility. Low could not be inferred from Saturn, affliction, fear, weakness, low energy, difficulty or the inverse of High.

## 4. Construct-first method

The round searched technical constructs before planetary significations. It examined Ptolemy, Dorotheus, Valens, Firmicus, and the Paulus/Olympiodorus/Rhetorius transmission, with reliable translations or scholarly reconstruction where available.

Each candidate was classified as direct initiation, direct advancement, direct pre-action restraint, nearby activity/enterprise/courage/impulse/delay/difficulty, outcome only, event timing only, non-P004 or unresolved. Only the three direct classes could proceed to fact feasibility.

## 5. Initiation finding

`katarche` is a genuine technical construct of beginning/inception. In Dorotheus it judges a matter from its commencement through its middle, end and outcome; in Ptolemaic usage “initiatives” are treated through temporal prediction of enterprises. That makes it an event/inception construct, not a stable natal behavioral preference.

Result: no `DIRECT_INITIATION` construct.

## 6. Advancement finding

`praxis` and lords of action describe occupation, pursuit, calling or the quality of work. Enterprise and accomplishment language describes work type, combinations or results. None isolates the process by which a native habitually advances an already-started concrete action.

Result: no `DIRECT_ADVANCEMENT` construct.

## 7. Pre-action restraint finding

Firmicus and related material can describe slowness, sluggishness, impediment or prolonged consideration. These passages describe difficulty, diminished activity or a general cognitive quality. They do not state a stable preference to observe, prepare, restrain or wait **before** concrete action.

Result: no `DIRECT_PRE_ACTION_RESTRAINT` construct.

## 8. Daimon/Spirit finding

The Lot of Daimon/Spirit is a real natal and timing construct associated with intentionality, mental action, reputation and outcomes. It is nevertheless too broad: willing or intentional action does not specify initiation rate, advancement style or pre-action restraint. Its calculability does not create semantic ownership.

Result: `NEARBY_ACTIVITY`, not direct P004 evidence.

## 9. Translation and context controls

English “action”, “initiative”, “active impulse” and “enterprise” were never accepted by word match alone. `Praxis` is controlled by its occupational context; `katarche` by its inceptional/event context. Dorotheus's transmission history and the preliminary status of the Riley Valens translation add further caution.

No cross-author vocabulary was flattened into an authorless rule.

## 10. Stable tendency versus event outcome

Inception charts, electional/horary-like questions, chronocrators, profections, annual results, enterprise success and planetary age periods were separated from natal personality. Even a direct reference to “beginning” fails P004 if it describes the chart of an event rather than the native's stable action preference.
