# Primitive Ontology v3: Task Continuity Pilot

## Boundary

`TP001 Task Continuity Process` asks how an undertaken matter is sustained,
revised, interrupted, or carried through after initiation. It excludes success,
diligence praise, initiation speed, organizational method, role, and outcome.

This discovery asset **does not replace v2**. Its status is `method-blocked`, its
runtime activation: prohibited, and it contributes no formal Core state.

## Why this pilot

Task continuity is closer to a behavior process repeatedly described by
traditional sources than P006's pre-set-structure preference. The candidate is
still only a research question: no source phrase, executable method, canonical
fact, Evidence Root, PRIMARY_EVIDENCE, or Mapping is admitted by this document.

## Advancement gate

Advancement requires an exact-source construct; person-subject ownership; a
reproducible system-native calculation; independent direction and
counterevidence; context containment; calibration and holdout; and explicit
approval. Until then, v2 remains the reproducible baseline and TP001 remains
isolated from all loaders and registries.
