## Role-eligibility notes

The following are role-eligible fact classes only; they are not admitted Mapping Candidates because no Primary mechanism exists to attach them to:

- Astrology aspects: `RULE_GATE` or `MODIFIER`.
- Astrology dignity and orb bands: `MODIFIER`.
- Astrology houses, angles, MC, and Ascendant: time-sensitive `CONTEXTUALIZER` or salience modifier.
- Bazi pillar position and visible/hidden provenance: `RULE_GATE` or provenance constraint.

No coverage quota applies. Zero Primary candidates for any Primitive, including P005, is an allowed and correct outcome at this stage.
