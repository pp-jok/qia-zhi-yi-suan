# Semantic Content Bazi Source Corpus

Status: `RESEARCH_CORPUS_CLOSED_WITHOUT_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`

## Scope and evidence boundary

This corpus tests the Zi Ping materials used in the deep review of P001, P002,
P003, P005, and P006. It asks whether a source supplies a construct-matched,
reproducible bridge from chart facts to the approved Primitive question. A
classical character word, a case outcome, or a modern resemblance is not that
bridge.

Every external traditional assertion in this corpus is classified as
`traditional_methodology`. It is not empirical validation of a modern
personality construct. `authority` identifies the historical work; `access
copy` identifies the public text used to locate a passage. An access copy is
not a second authority and does not repair an uncertain received text.

## Method result

The corpus repeatedly requires whole-chart judgement: day master, month
command, strength, roots, visible/hidden stems, production/control, pattern
formation, protection, rescue, and sometimes timing. The works are not one
interchangeable rulebook. A phrase from one school, a later commentary, and a
compendium entry cannot be averaged into a new authorless personality score.

The current canonical Bazi fact contract records pillars, hidden stems, Ten
Gods, and configured relations. It does not govern a reproducible method for
strength, month-command weighting, root/visibility priority, useful-god or
pattern selection, rescue, exception precedence, or timing. Therefore a
direct-looking phrase still cannot become executable evidence without both a
construct match and a governed method.

## Saturation and registry decision

The four named corpora, their accessible locators, Ten-God language,
five-phase temperament, month-command/pattern method, whole-chart commentary,
and counteraction/rescue vocabulary have been reviewed. Repeated candidates
remain temperament, role, structure, capability, or outcome rather than a
direct construct. This is `NO_EXECUTABLE_DIRECT_CONSTRUCT`.

No source registry, claim registry, Evidence Root, `PRIMARY_EVIDENCE`, or
Mapping is created from this corpus.
