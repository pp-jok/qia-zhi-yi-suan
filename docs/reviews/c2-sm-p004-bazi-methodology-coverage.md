## Coverage conclusion

The closure is not based on Ten Gods alone. It follows review of Ten-God structure, multi-factor combinations, month-command pattern formation, elemental temperament, whole-chart flow/obstruction and two modern structural treatments.

The reviewed systems are better evidenced here as languages of structural role, configuration, relation, condition and outcome than as a direct taxonomy of the P004 behavioral process. This is a scoped conclusion under the reviewed methods and corpus, not a permanent claim about all Bazi traditions.
